B10 CENTRED CAMERA BEAM

Print ONE B10_Camera_beam_centred_1x.stl at 100% scale in millimetres.
This replaces the powered camera pitch cradle/crossbeam. The camera and
its two mounting holes move 20 mm to the robot's left-right centreline.
The towers, pivot positions, linkage, lower M3 camera crank and plate stay
unchanged. The beam itself remains asymmetric to fit the existing towers.

PRINTING
The STL is pre-oriented at bed Z=0, about 57.1 x 138.5 x 18.6 mm.
PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle: 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers and 40%
infill are a starting point. Enable supports beneath elevated pivot and
arm features; inspect the slicer preview and clean out nut pockets.
The STL contains one watertight connected solid.

INSTALLATION
Reuse both M3 x 12 mm camera screws and their nuts. Camera-hole spacing
remains 32 mm, with 3.4 mm clearance holes and the existing nut pockets.
Install the nuts and camera on the new beam, then transfer the original
pivot hardware, sleeves and connecting rod. Preserve the servo's angular
indexing. Check free movement by hand before powered operation.
Nominal checked range: 10 degrees up to 65 degrees down.

CAMERA VIEW
The lens is on the robot centreline. Both front deck corners remain in
the nominal view at 40 degrees down. Part of one 6 mm calibration patch
crosses the conservative allowance for tall electronics/wiring. Keep
plugs and wires out of that sightline and confirm the actual camera view.

Use this STL instead of the original Camera_pitch_cradle from older B10
plates. Those previously exported slicer projects are not updated.
The Git repository's B10 Fusion and STEP assemblies include this beam.
