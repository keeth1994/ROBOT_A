# B10 CAD

Latest: [complete Fusion assembly](B10/Robot_B10.f3d), [STEP assembly](B10/Robot_B10.step), [standalone components](B10/parts), and [changes and checks](B10/README.md).

B10 includes the narrowed direct-drive robot, reinforced pillars, the user's edited top plate, and the elevated servo-driven camera. The camera beam now centres the lens on the robot's left-right centreline; its existing pivots and linkage remain in place. The camera remains driven by a Parallax 900-00005 with the M3-threaded metal horn and its replacement two-bolt crank. The assembled Fusion model retains 12 joints. Physical fit and loaded strength remain subject to hardware testing.

The measured interfaces, component generator and validation scope are documented in [B10](B10/README.md). Earlier B9-8 CAD exports are superseded and remain available in Git history. Local draft files are not part of the current tracked release. MuJoCo and its exporter remain tied to the preserved B9-1 assembly.
